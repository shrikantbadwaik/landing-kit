# Privacy Policy — Travel with Thikana

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026  
**Developer:** Shrikant Badwaik  
**Privacy contact:** [shrikant.badwaik@gmail.com](mailto:shrikant.badwaik@gmail.com)

Travel with Thikana is an Android travel companion that keeps arrival essentials available offline. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## 1. Information you save in the app

The app stores information you choose to enter or import, including:

- Destinations, arrival dates, accommodation names, addresses, booking references, and contact numbers.
- Transfer instructions, travel notes, emergency information, dietary phrases, and other arrival details.
- Checklist tasks, completion status, personal checklist templates, and readiness-check results.
- Ticket images, QR-code screenshots, PDFs, and their file metadata.
- Preferences such as arrival reminders and optional attachment protection.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not automatically upload your travel details or attachments to the developer or a cloud account. You do not need an account to use the app.

We use this information to display your trips, provide arrival assistance, maintain checklists, organise documents, and offer features you request.

## 2. Analytics

The app includes **Google Analytics for Firebase**, provided by Google, to help us understand app usage and improve the product.

The SDK can automatically collect app and device information, usage events, session information, and an app-instance identifier that distinguishes installations of the app. Analytics data can be sent to Google when an internet connection is available.

We do not send destinations, addresses, booking references, travel notes, checklist content, or attachments to Firebase Analytics. The app does not implement custom analytics events containing your travel content.

Advertising ID collection and advertising-personalisation signals are disabled in the app. The app does not display advertisements.

Analytics collection is enabled in the current version, and there is no in-app analytics opt-out setting. Analytics data is processed and retained through Google's services according to the applicable service settings and policies. Deleting a trip or uninstalling the app does not itself delete analytics data already sent to Google.

For more information, see:

- [Firebase privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Analytics data collection](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Permissions and device features

The app uses internet access and network-state information for analytics. Travel features remain available offline.

If you enable arrival reminders, the app may request notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally, and the app can restore their schedules after a device restart.

You select documents through Android's file picker or share files to the app through Android's Sharesheet. The app copies selected files into its private storage and does not modify the originals. It does not request broad access to all files or photos.

If you enable attachment protection, Android handles biometric or device screen-lock authentication. The app receives an authentication result; it does not receive or store your fingerprints, face data, PIN, or screen-lock password.

The app does not request access to your device's precise location, contacts, camera, or SMS inbox.

## 4. Sharing and other apps

Information can leave the app when you choose an action that uses another app or service:

- Sharing travel details sends the selected content to the recipient or app you choose.
- Opening a saved address in a map app passes that address to the map app.
- Opening the dialler or messaging app passes the selected contact number. Travel with Thikana does not automatically place the call or send the message.
- Copying an address places it on the Android clipboard.
- Opening a document in another app gives that app access to a decrypted viewing copy.
- Printing or exporting a PDF creates a copy of the travel details included in that document.
- Exporting a backup saves an encrypted file to the location you select. That location may belong to a cloud-storage provider.

Recipients and other apps handle the information under their own privacy practices. Copies you share, print, or export may remain outside Travel with Thikana after you delete the original trip.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Storage and security

Trip records, checklist information, attachment metadata, and preferences are kept in Android's app-private storage. These database records and preferences are not separately encrypted by the app.

Imported attachments and their saved thumbnails are encrypted using AES-GCM with a key managed by Android Keystore. Viewing an attachment can create a temporary decrypted file in the app's cache for use by a document viewer.

Optional attachment protection adds an authentication step before viewing documents. It does not encrypt the entire trip database or protect copies that you have already shared or exported.

Backups you explicitly create are encrypted using your chosen password. The password is used locally for backup and restore operations and is not sent to the developer. Keep the password safe; the developer cannot recover it for you.

Android's automatic app backup is disabled. The app does not provide automatic cloud synchronisation.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Trips, checklists, templates, and attachments remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's local data. Past trips and attachments do not expire automatically.

You can delete trips and attachments through the app. Deleting a trip removes its associated local checklist records, attachment copies, thumbnails, and viewing previews. Deleting an attachment removes that attachment's local encrypted copy, thumbnail, and viewing preview.

Clearing the app's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its local data. This does not remove original files you imported, exported backups, printed documents, shared copies, or data held by other apps and services.

Because your travel information is stored locally, the developer cannot remotely access or delete it for you.
